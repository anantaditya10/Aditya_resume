01 / About

What I do

I've spent 11+ years moving from mobile-first C#/XAML apps to full-stack TypeScript — the common thread is caring about the seam where frontend meets API: what the contract looks like, who owns which error, and whether it still holds up for someone using a screen reader on a bad connection.

Most recently that's meant leading frontend architecture for a banking payments dashboard, building a 40+ component design system for a healthcare marketplace, and shipping a reusable auth library that outlived the team that wrote it.
